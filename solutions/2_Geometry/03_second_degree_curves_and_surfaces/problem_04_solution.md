### Essential Formulas

**Standard Parabola Opening Along the $x$-Axis:**
A parabola with vertex at $(0, 0)$ and axis of symmetry along the $x$-axis ($y = 0$) has the equation:

$$y^2 = 4px$$

When $p > 0$, the parabola opens to the right (toward $+x$) and is defined only for $x \ge 0$.

---

### Problem Statement

**Exercise 4. Parabola $y^2 = 12x$**

Analyze the parabola

$$y^2 = 12x.$$

Find its vertex, axis of symmetry, opening direction, the points on the curve corresponding to $x = 3$, and make a labelled sketch.

> **Why this exercise:** connects the algebraic equation of a parabola with its symmetry, orientation, specific coordinate points, and geometric sketch.

---

### Solution

**Step 1: Identify the vertex, axis of symmetry, and opening direction**

Compare $y^2 = 12x$ with the canonical form $y^2 = 4px$:
* **Vertex:** Since there are no linear shift terms, the vertex is at the origin $V = (0, 0)$.
* **Axis of symmetry:** Because $y$ appears squared ($y^2 = (-y)^2$), the curve is symmetric about the **$x$-axis** ($y = 0$).
* **Opening direction:** Since $4p = 12 \implies p = 3 > 0$, and $y^2 \ge 0$ requires $12x \ge 0 \implies x \ge 0$, the parabola **opens to the right** (along the positive $x$-axis).

**Step 2: Find the points on the parabola for $x = 3$**

Substitute $x = 3$ into $y^2 = 12x$:

$$y^2 = 12(3) = 36 \implies y = \pm \sqrt{36} = \pm 6$$

This gives two symmetric points on the parabola:

$$P_1 = (3, 6), \qquad P_2 = (3, -6)$$

*(Note: Since $p = 3$, these two points are the endpoints of the latus rectum passing through the focus $F = (3, 0)$.)*

**Step 3: Labelled Sketch**

```text
                       y ^            * (3,6)
                         |         . '
                         |      . '
                         |   . '
                 Vertex  | .'       Focus
  -----------------------*------------*----------> x
                       (0,0)        (3,0)
                         | '.
                         |   ' .
                         |      ' .
                         |         ' .
                         |            * (3,-6)
```

---

### Final Answer

1. **Vertex:** $(0, 0)$.
2. **Axis of symmetry:** $x$-axis ($y = 0$).
3. **Opening direction:** Opens to the right (in the positive $x$-direction, $x \ge 0$).
4. **Points for $x = 3$:** $(3, 6)$ and $(3, -6)$.
